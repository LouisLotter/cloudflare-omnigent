# Your account

Your Omnigent account is your username and password. It sits behind the installation's Cloudflare sign-in, so reaching Omnigent takes both: the emailed code (remembered in your browser for a day), then your password.

## Changing your password

Open **Settings → Account** and choose **Change password**. Enter your current password, then the new one twice.

The computers pages have a link at the top, **Omnigent password**. For a member it opens a short page that sends you here; the form on that page is for the installation owner's own password only.

## A forgotten password

Ask an owner or administrator of your team. On the Members page they choose **Reset** next to your name, and Omnigent shows them a new password, once, to give you. The reset also signs you out everywhere. Sign in with the new password, then change it to one of your own under Settings → Account.

Nobody can see your current password, administrators included. That is why a reset gives you a new one rather than telling you the old one.

## A suspended membership

An owner or administrator can suspend a membership, for example while someone is away or if an account may be misused. While you are suspended, Omnigent says "Your membership of this team is suspended. Ask a team administrator." and the computers pages refuse you. Your sessions, computers and files are kept, and your scheduled tasks pause.

When they reinstate you, sign in again in your browser. Your computers are kept too, and start again the next time you use them.
